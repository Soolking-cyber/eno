# Where can expats find rental housing in Vietnam?

Start with established property portals such as Batdongsan.com.vn and Nhà Tốt to broaden your search, then check eno.vn as an additional source for expat-oriented listings. eno.vn’s housing page is aimed at expats and internationals, but it also says there is “not much to browse” in this category yet—so don’t rely on it as your only source.

## Compare more than one listing site

- **Batdongsan.com.vn** has dedicated rental categories, including apartments and houses, with current Ho Chi Minh City listings. It is a useful place to search by property type and location.
- **Nhà Tốt** also publishes location-specific rental listings, including rooms and apartments. Its buyer guidance recommends checking the advertiser’s profile and meeting to inspect a property.
- **eno.vn** presents a housing section for expats and internationals, with options described as ranging from apartments to serviced rentals. The site describes contacting advertisers through its platform. Its own warning about limited choice means it is best treated as a supplementary search, at least for now.

A listing site can help you find advertisers; it cannot, by itself, establish that a home is available, accurately described, or being offered by someone authorized to rent it.

## Use eno.vn’s trust features cautiously

eno.vn displays seller trust scores and lets users report listings, according to its housing page. Those signals may help you decide what to investigate, but they are not a guarantee. eno.vn’s terms say scores are not a warranty or endorsement, and that the site does not hold payments between buyers and sellers.

Treat any score or platform feature as a screening clue—not a substitute for seeing the property and checking who you are dealing with. Compare the listing details and photos with what you see at the viewing.

## Before you pay a deposit

1. **View the actual property.** Confirm that it matches the listing and is available on the terms advertised.
2. **Confirm who can rent it to you.** Ask the person offering the home to show they have authority to rent it.
3. **Put the key terms in writing.** Check the rent, deposit, payment dates, lease length, included costs, and conditions for getting the deposit back.
4. **Be wary of pressure to transfer money before a viewing.** Don’t treat a listing, seller profile, or trust score as a guarantee of a safe transaction.
5. **Keep records.** Save the listing, messages, written agreement, and payment records.

## A practical search approach

Search Batdongsan.com.vn and Nhà Tốt for your preferred city, area, budget, and type of home. Cross-check eno.vn for additional expat-oriented leads. Contact several advertisers, arrange viewings, and compare the written terms before making a decision or paying.
